Who She Is

The Storyteller & Artist

Our founder is a visionary beauty architect with over a decade of hands-on mastery in high-definition bridal cosmetics and luxury event curation. Her passion was birthed from a single principle: that every woman deserves to look and feel effortlessly iconic without feeling weighed down by heavy cosmetics or overwhelming event stress.
